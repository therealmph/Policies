SpaMote Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-18

Data collection

SpaMote is a local remote for existing compatible ScreenLogic pool and spa systems. It does not require an account or operate a cloud service. SpaMote does not automatically send personal information, equipment readings, or usage data to its developer.

SpaMote contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. It does not sell your data or track you across apps or websites.

Data that stays on your iPhone

SpaMote saves the controller's local address and port so it can reconnect. It also saves appearance and temperature preferences, dashboard and feature assignments, previously observed heat modes, and light-program usage counts used to order controls.

Equipment names, temperatures, operating states, and settings are read from your controller to display and control your system. SpaMote does not upload these readings to its developer.

Local Network access

Local Network permission allows SpaMote to discover and communicate directly with your ScreenLogic adapter on your local network. It searches when you request discovery and reconnects to a saved controller when you use the app or a widget control.

You can revoke this permission in iPhone Settings. Discovery, readings, and equipment commands will then be unavailable. SpaMote does not provide internet remote access or relay your equipment connection through a developer-operated server.

Widget storage

To support Home Screen widgets, SpaMote stores a local cache of Pool/Spa states, temperatures and targets, light counts, the last-read time, and display preferences in an App Group shared only with its own widget extension. A random connection identifier prevents widgets from targeting an earlier saved connection.

This shared cache does not contain the controller's network address, household or circuit names, passwords, or adapter identifiers. Widget readings can remain cached while the app is closed. A widget command requests a controller read before applying a change.

Diagnostics you choose to share

You may export a redacted diagnostic report through the iPhone share sheet. Nothing is sent until you choose a destination and share it. The report contains app/build and operating-system versions, runtime and connection status, and limited snapshot timing and counts.

The report omits controller and circuit names, full network addresses, passwords, and adapter identifiers. Review it before sharing. The recipient or service you choose handles that shared copy under its own policies. If you send information directly to the developer, include only what you intend the developer to receive.

Retention and deletion

Forget Controller removes the saved controller address and connection identifier, dashboard and body-feature assignments, remembered heat modes, and cached widget equipment readings. Your appearance and temperature preferences and light-program usage counts remain on the phone.

Removing the app deletes its private app storage. iOS manages shared widget storage and may include app settings in device backups, depending on your Apple backup settings. Manage those backups through Apple's settings; SpaMote does not operate a backup service.

Equipment settings and schedules are stored on the pool controller. Forgetting a controller or removing SpaMote does not turn equipment off, cancel schedules, or erase controller settings. Manage those settings through your controller or another trusted client.

Apple services

Apple distributes the app and may process App Store, device-backup, and operating-system diagnostic information according to your Apple settings and Apple's policies. These platform services are separate from SpaMote's local equipment connection.

Questions about this policy

For privacy questions or bug reports, contact SpaMote's developer, GitHub user therealmph, through the Policies repository's issue tracker. Ongoing technical support, response times, and resolution are not guaranteed.

GitHub issues are public. Do not include household details, network addresses, passwords, adapter identifiers, or photos that reveal personal information. Review any diagnostic report before posting it. Opening the contact or online policy links visits GitHub-hosted pages; GitHub handles website visitor and account information under its own privacy statement. A GitHub account may be needed to submit an issue.
